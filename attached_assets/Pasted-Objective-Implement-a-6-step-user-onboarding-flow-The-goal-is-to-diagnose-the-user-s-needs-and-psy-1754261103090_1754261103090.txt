Objective:
Implement a 6-step user onboarding flow. The goal is to diagnose the user's needs and psychologically frame Runway AI as the essential tool for their success, leading them to a personalized paywall to start a free trial.

Technical Requirements:

Display one question per screen for a focused, premium feel.

Store the user's answer from each question to be used in subsequent questions and the final paywall screen.

The design should be clean, minimal, and mobile-first.

Onboarding Flow Implementation Details:
Screen 1: The Dream

Question: "What is the exact title you are training to win?"

Input Type: Text Field

Placeholder Text: e.g., Miss Universe, Miss Teen USA, Miss California

Variable to Store: [end_goal]

Screen 2: The Experience

Question: "How many pageants have you competed in?"

Input Type: Multiple Choice Buttons

Choices:

"Zero, I'm just starting out."

"1-3, I'm learning the ropes."

"4+, I'm a seasoned competitor."

Variable to Store: [experience_level]

Screen 3: The Strategy

Question: "To win [end_goal], which of these needs the most work right now?"

Input Type: Multiple Choice Buttons

Choices:

"My Onstage Walk & Presence"

"My Interview & Q&A Skills"

"My Overall Prep & Scheduling"

"My Wardrobe & Styling"

Variable to Store: [focus_area]

Screen 4: The Timeline

Question: "When is your next competition?"

Input Type: Multiple Choice Buttons

Choices:

"Less than 1 month"

"1-3 months"

"3-6 months"

"I haven't scheduled one yet"

Variable to Store: [timeline]

Screen 5: The Motivation

Question: "Winning [end_goal] is a launchpad. What is the main thing the crown unlocks for you?"

Input Type: Multiple Choice Buttons

Choices:

"A platform for my social cause"

"A career in modeling or entertainment"

"Scholarships and academic opportunities"

"The confidence to take on any challenge"

Variable to Store: [motivation]

Screen 6: The Gap

Question: "A single hour with a top-tier pageant coach can cost over $250. How are you getting that level of expertise?"

Input Type: Multiple Choice Buttons

Choices:

"I'm currently working with a coach."

"I'm looking for the right coach."

"I'm learning from free sources (YouTube, etc.)."

"I'm not sure where to start."

Variable to Store: [coach_status]
