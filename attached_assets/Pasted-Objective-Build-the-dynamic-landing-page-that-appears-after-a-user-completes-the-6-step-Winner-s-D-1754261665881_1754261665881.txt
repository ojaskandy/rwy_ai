Objective:
Build the dynamic landing page that appears after a user completes the 6-step "Winner's Diagnosis" onboarding flow. This page's sole purpose is to convert the user by presenting a personalized plan and a compelling call-to-action to start a free trial.

Trigger:
This page loads immediately after the user answers the final onboarding question ("How are you getting that level of expertise?").

Data Requirements:
This page must have access to the variables collected during the onboarding flow:

[end_goal]

[timeline]

[focus_area]

[coach_status]

Page Structure & Implementation Details:
1. The Personalized Headline

Component: H1 or equivalent large, bold heading.

Content: "Your Game Plan for [end_goal] is Ready."

Logic: Dynamically insert the [end_goal] variable.

2. The Diagnostic Summary

Component: A visually distinct section (e.g., a slightly off-white card or a bordered box) with a 3-column layout.

Logic: Display the following key-value pairs using the stored variables.

Column 1:

Label: YOUR GOAL

Value: [end_goal]

Column 2:

Label: YOUR TIMELINE

Value: [timeline]

Column 3:

Label: PRIMARY FOCUS

Value: [focus_area]

3. The First Mission

Component: A focused content block to direct user action.

Headline: "Your First Mission: Master Your [focus_area]"

Body Copy: "Based on your timeline, the single most impactful thing you can do right now is improve your [focus_area]. We'll start you with our AI-powered [Mapped Feature] to see immediate improvement."

Logic: Implement a mapping function to insert the correct feature name based on the [focus_area] variable:

If [focus_area] is "My Onstage Walk & Presence", [Mapped Feature] is "Live Routine Coach".

If [focus_area] is "My Interview & Q&A Skills", [Mapped Feature] is "Interview Simulator".

If [focus_area] is "My Wardrobe & Styling", [Mapped Feature] is "Virtual Dress Try-On".

If [focus_area] is "My Overall Prep & Scheduling", [Mapped Feature] is "Competition Calendar".
